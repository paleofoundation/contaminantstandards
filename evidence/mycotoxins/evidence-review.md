# Mycotoxins: Evidence Review

Evidence review, version 0.1, 2026-11-02

Status: working draft for owner review. Not a standard. All values were read from the cited source on 2026-11-02; source tags [S#] map to rows in `sources.csv` in this folder. Values not confirmed in a source are marked "not found".

Conventions used in this document:

- EU texts print decimals with a comma and thousands with a space (for example "5,0" and "1 000"). They are written here as "5.0" and "1000". No other unit conversion, rounding or averaging has been applied. Units are reported exactly as each source states them (ug/kg, ppb, ppm, mg/kg, ug/L).
- "ug" is used for micrograms.

## 1. Scope

### 1.1 Analytes

- Aflatoxins B1, B2, G1, G2 (individually and as total aflatoxins) and aflatoxin M1
- Ochratoxin A (OTA)
- Deoxynivalenol (DON)
- Fumonisins (B1 + B2; note that FDA guidance uses FB1 + FB2 + FB3)
- Zearalenone (ZEN)
- Patulin
- Citrinin
- T-2 and HT-2 toxins (sum)

### 1.2 Matrices

Foods: cereals and cereal products (including oats and maize), groundnuts (peanuts), tree nuts, dried fruit (including figs and vine fruit), spices, milk and infant formula, apple juice and apple products, coffee, cocoa, wine and grape juice, baby food and processed cereal-based food for infants and young children.

Consumer products where evidence was found: food supplements based on red yeast rice (citrinin), pet food (dog and cat food), and animal feed as an upstream matrix.

Regulatory method-performance requirements (EU official control) [S13], Annex II, point 4.2.1.1, Table 1:

- Aflatoxin B1 in baby food, processed cereal-based food and FSMP for infants and young children: LOQ <= 0.1 ug/kg.
- Each of aflatoxins B1, B2, G1, G2 in all other foods: LOQ <= 1 ug/kg.
- Ochratoxin A in cocoa powder: LOQ <= 3.0 ug/kg; in liquorice confectionery containing < 97% liquorice extract: LOQ <= 10.0 ug/kg.
- All other cases: LOQ shall be <= 0.5 x ML and should preferably be <= 0.2 x ML; for sum-MLs, each individual toxin LOQ <= 0.5 x ML/n.

US federal program requirements [S14]: laboratories use the multi-mycotoxin method C-003 or an appropriate AOAC OMA method; qualitative tests are not acceptable; LC-UV/FLD results require separate MS confirmation; results between LOD and LOQ are reported as trace.

## 5. Gaps

- EU consolidated text: the version read is dated 01.07.2025. A later consolidation or amendment (2025 to 2026) was not checked.
- FDA: CPG Sec. 555.400 was reissued as final guidance in June 2021 (per search results); its current text was not opened (the old URL returned 404). The 20 ppb value is taken from FDA's 2000 action-levels guidance [S2], whose page says it is current as of August 2000.
- FDA has no published action, guidance or advisory level found for ochratoxin A, zearalenone, T-2/HT-2 or citrinin in human food.
- Pet food: no pet-food-specific limit was found in any jurisdiction read. The FDA feed action level for "animal species or uses not specified above" [S2] is the nearest US value; treating it as covering pet food is an interpretation, not a stated rule. EU feed limits (Directive 2002/32/EC, Recommendation 2006/576/EC) were not opened.
- Supplements: the only supplement-specific limit found is EU citrinin in red yeast rice supplements [S1]. No US supplement limit was found.
- Prop 65: no NSRL found for aflatoxins, ochratoxin A or fumonisin B1 [S9]; the OEHHA NSRL table page returned no content to the fetch tool.
- Occurrence: EFSA abstracts give sample counts but not detection rates; full EFSA opinions were not mined. No US federal occurrence summary (FDA TDS, CP 7307.001 results, USDA) was found. Several surveys are non-US and use ELISA (Serbia milk) or convenience samples (FDA DON survey).
- Methods: most EN/ISO full texts are paywalled; only publicly visible scope/abstract text was used. LOQs for EN 14177, EN 15851, EN 14132, ISO 16050 and AOAC methods were not found.
- Prices: public price lists found are mostly feed and agricultural labs; no public consumer-product (food or supplement) mycotoxin price list was found for large commercial labs (Eurofins, Romer, SGS, Medallion).
