# ICS Draft Standard: Bisphenols (BPA and analogs)

**DRAFT, not for certification use**

Institute of Contaminant Standards (ICS), contaminantstandards.com. Version 0.1, 2026-11-02.

All [S#] tags reference `../../evidence/bisphenols/sources.csv`. Values not sourced in the evidence review are marked "TBD" for owner decision. No value in this draft is a policy decision.

## 1. Scope (DRAFT)

This draft covers testing and certification of products for the presence or migration of bisphenols:

- Foods: canned foods and beverages, infant formula.
- Consumer products: food contact materials (plastics, varnishes and coatings, adhesives, rubbers, ion-exchange resins, printing inks, silicones, as listed in Reg. (EU) 2024/3190 Art. 1(2) [S1]), thermal paper, baby bottles, toys.

Certification under this standard states measured results against stated limits. It does not make health claims. Claims such as "BPA-free", "safe" or "non-toxic" are outside the scope of any ICS certificate. Permitted wording is limited to "below the stated limit" or "not detected at LOQ X (method, laboratory)".

## 2. Definitions (DRAFT)

- **Bisphenol**: "a substance consisting of two hydroxyphenyl functional groups linked by one bridging atom" (Reg. (EU) 2024/3190 Art. 2) [S1].
- **Hazardous bisphenol**: a bisphenol or bisphenol derivative listed in Part 3 of Annex VI to Reg. (EC) No 1272/2008 (Reg. (EU) 2024/3190 Art. 2) [S1].
- **Specific migration limit (SML)**: maximum permitted amount of a substance released from a material or article into food or food simulants (Reg. (EU) 2018/213 Art. 1) [S3].
- **LOD / LOQ**: limit of detection and limit of quantification as reported by the accredited laboratory for the specific analyte, matrix and method.
- **TECRID**: ICS's persistent identifier for a versioned laboratory evidence record.
- **Applicant**: the party seeking certification of a product.

## 9. Reporting and TECRID publication (DRAFT)

- Each certificate's laboratory report is published as a TECRID record.
- Each TECRID record shall contain at minimum (proposed fields, TBD for owner confirmation): laboratory name and ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation number; method reference; analyte list; matrix; simulant and test conditions (for migration); LOD and LOQ per analyte; result per analyte as reported (numeric or "not detected at LOQ X"); sample identification; dates of sampling and analysis; report version.
- Result wording on certificates is limited to "below the stated limit" or "not detected at LOQ X (source)". No health claims and no "BPA-free" claims.
- Revisions to a laboratory report create a new TECRID version; earlier versions remain accessible (versioning rules TBD).
