# ICS Acrylamide Standard (draft skeleton)

Institute of Contaminant Standards (ICS), contaminantstandards.com. Class: ACRYLAMIDE. Slug: acrylamide.
Version 0.1, 2026-11-02. Source tags [S#] refer to `../../evidence/acrylamide/sources.csv`. Supporting evidence is in `../../evidence/acrylamide/evidence-review.md`.

## 1. Scope (DRAFT)

1.1 This draft covers certification of finished products for acrylamide content in these matrices: french fries, potato crisps/chips and other potato dough products, bread, breakfast cereals, biscuits and crackers (including crispbread and gingerbread), coffee and coffee substitutes, and baby foods and processed cereal-based foods for infants and young children. These follow the food categories of Regulation (EU) 2017/2158, Article 1(2) [S1].

1.2 Consumer products are in scope only where a sourced numeric value exists:
- drinking water treatment (residual acrylamide monomer) [S13] [S22]
- cosmetic products containing polyacrylamides (residual acrylamide) [S23]

1.3 Out of scope: glycidamide (mentioned only as context [S16]), food contact materials, occupational exposure, and human biomonitoring.

1.4 Certification under this standard is a statement about measured acrylamide content relative to stated reference values. It makes no claim about health outcomes.

## 2. Definitions (DRAFT)

- **Acrylamide:** 2-propenamide, CAS 79-06-1 [S9].
- **Benchmark level:** a performance indicator used to verify whether acrylamide mitigation measures are effective, as set out in Annex IV of Regulation (EU) 2017/2158. It is not a maximum level [S1].
- **Maximum level:** a legally binding upper limit in a regulation. None exists for acrylamide in food in Regulation (EU) 2023/915 as of the 19.08.2026 consolidation [S3].
- **Product type:** groups of products with the same or similar ingredients, recipe design, process design and/or process controls, where these may affect acrylamide levels in the finished product. This wording follows Annex III, Part I, point 3 of Regulation (EU) 2017/2158 [S2].
- **LOQ / LOD:** limit of quantification / limit of detection, as reported by the accredited laboratory for the specific matrix.
- **TECRID:** ICS's persistent identifier for versioned laboratory evidence records. Each certificate's laboratory report is published as a TECRID record.
- **Candidate reference value:** a sourced regulatory or benchmark value listed in Section 7 for the standards owner to consider. It is not an adopted ICS threshold until the owner approves it.

## 3. Analytes (DRAFT)

3.1 Primary analyte: acrylamide (CAS 79-06-1) [S9].

3.2 Reporting basis:
- Foods: µg/kg of product as sold, matching the units of Annex IV [S1].
- Coffee and coffee substitutes: dry product, matching Annex IV categories "roast coffee", "instant (soluble) coffee" and "coffee substitutes" [S1]. EFSA occurrence data for coffee are reported on the solid form [S16].
- Drinking water: µg/l [S22].
- Cosmetics: mg/kg residual acrylamide [S23].

3.3 Glycidamide: not an analyte in this draft. TBD by the owner.

## 5. Sampling (DRAFT)

5.1 Food sampling follows Regulation (EU) 2017/2158, Annex III, Part I [S2]:
- The sample shall be representative of the sampled batch.
- A representative sample of each product type shall be taken.
- Monitoring shall prioritize product types with demonstrated potential to exceed the benchmark level.

5.2 Frequency reference: Annex III, Part III requires food business operators to sample and analyze at least annually for products with a known and well-controlled acrylamide level, and more often for products that may exceed the benchmark level [S2]. ICS certification frequency is TBD (see Section 10).

5.3 Official-control sampling reference: Commission Recommendation (EU) 2019/1888 points Member States to Part B of the Annex to Regulation (EC) No 333/2007. It notes that FBO sampling may deviate from that procedure but should remain representative of the lot [S4]. Regulation (EC) No 333/2007 itself was not reviewed for this draft. TBD.

5.4 Sample size, increments, and chain-of-custody requirements: TBD. No sourced numeric values in the evidence review.

## 6. Accredited laboratory and method requirements (DRAFT)

6.1 Laboratories shall hold ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation with acrylamide in the relevant matrix within the accredited scope.

6.2 Laboratories shall take part in suitable proficiency testing schemes and shall show internal quality control procedures. Certified reference materials should be used where possible. These follow Annex III, Part II, point 2 of 2017/2158 [S2].

6.3 Candidate methods (owner to select):
- EN 16618:2015, LC-ESI-MS/MS, for bakery ware, potato products and roasted coffee. Validated from 14,3 µg/kg to 9 083 µg/kg; LOQ expected between below 15 µg/kg and 30 µg/kg [S19].
- ISO 18862:2025, HPLC-MS/MS or GC-MS after derivatization, for coffee and coffee products. Validated from 53 µg/kg to 612,1 µg/kg [S20].
- Other validated LC-MS/MS or GC-MS methods meeting 6.4.

6.4 Minimum performance criteria, from Annex III, Part II, point 3 of 2017/2158 [S2]:

| Parameter | Criterion |
|---|---|
| Recovery | 75-110% |
| LOD | three tenths of LOQ |
| LOQ, benchmark level below 125 µg/kg | at most two fifths of the benchmark level (not required to be lower than 20 µg/kg) |
| LOQ, benchmark level of 125 µg/kg or more | at most 50 µg/kg |
| Field blanks | below LOD |
| Repeatability (RSDr) | 0,66 times RSDR as derived from (modified) Horwitz equation |
| Reproducibility (RSDR) | as derived from (modified) Horwitz equation |
| Specificity | no matrix or spectral interferences |

6.5 Drinking water: measurement uncertainty at most 30% of the parametric value [S22].

6.6 Result treatment: Annex III, Part IV of 2017/2158 compares results corrected for recovery, without taking measurement uncertainty into account [S2]. Whether ICS adopts this rule or a measurement-uncertainty decision rule is TBD by the owner.

## 9. Reporting and TECRID publication (DRAFT)

9.1 Each certificate's laboratory report shall be published as a TECRID record.

9.2 The TECRID record shall include, as minimum fields:
- product and lot identifiers
- matrix code (Section 4)
- laboratory name and ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation number
- method reference
- LOQ and LOD for the matrix
- recovery
- result in the unit of Section 3.2, and whether the result is recovery-corrected
- measurement uncertainty
- sample receipt and analysis dates
- the candidate reference value and [S#] source applied

9.3 Results below LOQ shall be reported as "not detected at LOQ X µg/kg (laboratory, method)". They shall not be reported as zero or with absence wording.

9.4 Public claims shall use the form "below the stated level of X µg/kg (source)". Health claims are not permitted.

9.5 Versioning: a new TECRID version is created for each recertification. Prior versions remain accessible. Retention period TBD.

The only sourced reference is that 2017/2158 Annex III requires food business operators to sample and analyze at least annually for products with known and well-controlled acrylamide levels, more often for products that may exceed the benchmark level, and again after product or process changes that could alter acrylamide levels [S2].

10.2 Options for owner decision (no option adopted):
- Option 1: annual retest per product type, aligned with the Annex III minimum [S2].
