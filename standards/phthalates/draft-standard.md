# ICS Phthalates Standard (DRAFT)

**DRAFT, not for certification use**

Version 0.1, 2026-11-02. Institute of Contaminant Standards (ICS), contaminantstandards.com. All [S#] tags refer to `../../evidence/phthalates/sources.csv`. Every numeric value in this draft is copied from the evidence review (`../../evidence/phthalates/evidence-review.md`). Values not present there are marked TBD.

## 1. Scope (DRAFT)

1.1 This standard sets out how ICS would evaluate laboratory evidence on ortho-phthalate content or migration for:
- foods: fatty foods, dairy, edible oils, infant foods, prepared and fast foods;
- consumer products: toys and childcare articles, plastic food contact materials, cosmetics.

1.2 This standard does not make or support health claims or absence claims. Certification statements are limited to "below the stated limit" or "not detected at LOQ X (source)".

1.3 Non-phthalate plasticizers (for example DEHT and DINCH) are outside the scope of acceptance thresholds and may be reported as context only.

## 2. Definitions (DRAFT)

- **Phthalate (ortho-phthalate):** a diester of 1,2-benzenedicarboxylic acid, as listed in section 3.
- **Plasticised material:** as defined in REACH Annex XVII entry 51 paragraph 5 [S3].
- **Toy; child care article:** as defined in the instrument relevant to the target market, for example 16 CFR 1307 [S1], SOR/2016-188 section 1 [S10], or REACH Annex XVII entry 51 [S3].
- **SML / SML(T):** specific migration limit and total specific migration limit, in mg/kg, as in Regulation (EU) No 10/2011 Annex I [S5] [S20].
- **LOQ:** lowest concentration of target in a test sample that can be quantitatively determined with acceptable precision and accuracy under the method's conditions (ISO 8124-6:2023 clause 3.8 [S14]).
- **TECRID:** the ICS persistent identifier for a versioned laboratory evidence record (see section 9).
- **Candidate reference value:** a regulatory value from the evidence review that the standards owner may adopt, modify, or reject as an acceptance threshold.

## 5. Sampling (DRAFT)

5.1 Toys and childcare articles: test portion selection per ISO 8124-6:2023 clause 7 (individual test, composite test with K less than or equal to 3 under the conditions of clause 7.3) [S14], or per CPSC-CH-C1001-09.4 sample preparation [S2].

5.2 Plastic food contact materials: sampling and migration testing conditions per Regulation (EU) No 10/2011 (Annex V conditions referenced in [S5]); detailed plan TBD.

5.3 Foods: no sourced sampling standard in the evidence review. TBD.

5.4 Number of samples, lots, and retail versus factory sampling: TBD (owner decision).

## 6. Accredited laboratory and method requirements (DRAFT)

6.1 Testing laboratories shall hold ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation with the phthalate method within the accredited scope. (Accreditation status of individual labs listed in the evidence review was not checked.)

6.2 Accepted methods (candidate list, sourced):
- Toys and childcare articles: CPSC-CH-C1001-09.4 or the other standalone methods accepted by CPSC staff (EN 14372:2004, EN 71-5:1993/A1:2006, ASTM D7823-16, ASTM D8133-17, GB/T 22048-2015, ISO 8124-6, ISO 14389:2014 for textiles, Health Canada C34 and C34.2), modified to include all phthalates of interest [S2]; ISO 8124-6:2023 [S14].
- Foods: no standardized food method with a published LOQ was found. A research GC/MS method with published MDLs exists [S13]. TBD.
- Cosmetics and FCM migration: TBD.

6.3 LOQ requirement: TBD. Reference points from the evidence review: ISO 8124-6:2023 permits composite testing only when the LOQ is lower than 50 mg/kg [S14]; EFSA excluded results whose LOQ exceeded the EU SML [S12]. Candidate rule for owner decision: the reported LOQ shall not exceed the applicable acceptance threshold (value TBD).

6.4 Blanks and background contamination: each batch shall include a laboratory reagent blank [S2]. Blank acceptance criteria: TBD. Basis: phthalates are a common laboratory contaminant [S2] and were detected in at least half of laboratory method blanks in a published food survey [S13].

6.5 Recovery and calibration acceptance: CPSC-CH-C1001-09.4 requires a CRM result within ±15% of the expected value for calibration [S2]. Recovery criteria for foods: TBD.

## 9. Reporting and TECRID publication (DRAFT)

9.1 Each certificate's laboratory report is published as a TECRID record. A TECRID is ICS's persistent identifier for a versioned laboratory evidence record.

9.2 Each TECRID record shall include, at minimum: laboratory name and accreditation reference; method identifier and version; matrix and sample description; each analyte result with unit; LOQ per analyte; blank results; recovery or CRM results; date of analysis; and the [S#] reference for any threshold applied.

9.3 Results below LOQ shall be reported as "not detected at LOQ X", with X and its unit. No absence claims or health wording.

9.4 A corrected or reissued report creates a new TECRID version; earlier versions remain accessible. Versioning rules beyond this: TBD.
