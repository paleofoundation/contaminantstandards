# Pesticide Residues (glyphosate and AMPA, 2,4-D, atrazine, organophosphates, pyrethroids)

Evidence review, version 0.1, 2026-11-02

Status: DRAFT working evidence file for the Institute of Contaminant Standards (ICS). Not reviewed, not approved, not for certification use. Every number carries a source tag [S#] that maps to `sources.csv` in this folder. Values that could not be opened and confirmed in a source are recorded as "not found" (Section 7).

## 1. Purpose and reading notes

- This file collects regulatory limits, occurrence data and method information for a defined set of pesticide residues. It does not make any health or safety statement about any food, and it does not convert units between jurisdictions.
- Units are reported exactly as the source states them: US EPA tolerances in parts per million (ppm) [S1], EU MRLs in mg/kg [S14], Codex MRLs in mg/kg [S27], US drinking water MCLs in mg/L [S13], and Proposition 65 regulatory levels (NSRL and MADL) in micrograms per day [S36].
- EU values marked with `*` are shown exactly as the EU Pesticides Database publishes them (the database flags these values as set at the limit of analytical determination) [S14].
- US EPA tolerances apply to the residue definition stated in each CFR section (for example, glyphosate under 40 CFR 180.364(a)(1) is measured as glyphosate only, while 180.364(a)(2) is measured as glyphosate plus N-acetyl-glyphosate) [S1]. Residue definitions differ between the US, EU and Codex and must be compared with care.

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Analytes

| Group | Analytes in scope |
|---|---|
| Phosphonate herbicide | Glyphosate; AMPA (aminomethylphosphonic acid, glyphosate degradate) |
| Other herbicides | 2,4-D; atrazine |
| Organophosphate insecticides (representative set) | Chlorpyrifos; malathion; diazinon; acephate (plus methamidophos where it arises from acephate use [S7]) |
| Pyrethroid insecticides (representative set) | Bifenthrin; cypermethrin (including alpha- and zeta-cypermethrin); permethrin; lambda-cyhalothrin |

### 2.2 Foods and consumer products (candidate matrices)

Oats and oat products; wheat; soybeans; apples; strawberries; spinach; lettuce and leafy greens; foods for infants and young children (occurrence only, limits not verified in this version); drinking water (atrazine, 2,4-D, glyphosate MCLs only). Consumer products other than food and drinking water are out of scope for version 0.1.

## 6. Gaps

1. EU cypermethrin values are scheduled to change: the database lists "Not yet applicable" values under PLAN/2023/1863: apples 0.01*, oat 0.4, wheat 0.15, soyabeans 0.05, spinaches 0.01*, lettuces 0.01*, strawberries 0.07 mg/kg [S22]. Application date not found.
2. US PDP 2024 did not test oats, wheat, soybeans, strawberries or spinach; apples and lettuce are the only overlapping matrices in this review [S40, p. viii]. Glyphosate does not appear in PDP 2024 Appendix B (no glyphosate occurrence data from PDP 2024) [S40].
3. FDA FY2024 Table 3 gives counts of samples with residues per pesticide but not the number of human food samples analyzed for each pesticide, so detection rates per analyte could not be derived from the source [S41].
4. EU drinking water parametric values for pesticides, EU infant food limits (Directive 2006/125/EC and successor rules), and national limits for Canada, the UK and Japan were not compiled.
5. Published LOQs for EN 15662:2018 and for QuPPe-PO version 13 were not found in the material reviewed; laboratory-specific LOQs will be needed.
6. Codex sampling guideline CXG 33-1999 could not be opened on the official Codex site; it is not cited as verified.
7. The PDP 2024 tolerance column lists chlorpyrifos on apples as 0.1 FH, while the current eCFR lists 0.01 ppm for apple [S40, App. B p. 19] [S4]; PDP notes its tolerance column applies to 2024 [S40, App. B intro]. Needs owner review.
8. EFSA 2024 values were read from the Wiley full text HTML; a PDF copy was not obtained from EFSA directly.
