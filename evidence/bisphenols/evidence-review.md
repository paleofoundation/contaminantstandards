# Bisphenols (BPA and analogs): Evidence Review

Institute of Contaminant Standards (ICS), contaminantstandards.com

Evidence review, version 0.1, 2026-11-02

Status: working draft for owner review. Not a standard, not a certification basis. Every number carries an [S#] tag that maps to `sources.csv` in this folder. Values that could not be opened and confirmed in a source are recorded as "not found" (Section 7).

## 2. Scope

Class: bisphenols. Analytes in scope: bisphenol A (BPA, CAS 80-05-7 [S7]) and the analogs bisphenol S (BPS, 4,4'-dihydroxydiphenyl sulphone), bisphenol F (BPF), bisphenol AF (BPAF), bisphenol B (BPB) and bisphenol Z (BPZ). BADGE and BFDGE (bisphenol A and bisphenol F diglycidyl ethers) are included as context only.

Matrices: foods (canned foods and beverages, infant formula) and consumer products (food contact materials, thermal paper, baby bottles, toys).

## 6. Gaps

1. EU primary text access: 2024/3190 and 2026/250 were read through a mirror [S1] [S2], not EUR-Lex. Annex III content of 2024/3190 was not read.
2. No numeric migration limit for BPS, BPF, BPAF, BPB or BPZ was found in force in the EU after 2024/3190; the BPS SML entry in 10/2011 was deleted [S1] [S4]. Whether BPAF or other analogs are "hazardous bisphenols" under Art. 2 depends on CLP Annex VI Part 3 listings, which were not checked.
3. No public EURL-FCM method or its LOQ for bisphenols at 1 microgram/kg was retrieved.
4. Occurrence data are dated (2008 to 2021) and come from abstracts. No post-2024 EU surveillance data on BPA or analogs after the EU ban were found.
5. EFSA 2015 occurrence and exposure tables were not opened.
6. FDA Total Diet Study bisphenol results were not found.
7. Prop 65 listing date for BPA could not be read on OEHHA (site blocked the tool); only the DARTIC meeting date of May 7, 2015 is sourced [S15].
8. China: only one GB 9685 additive entry (FCA0340) was read in translation [S19]; the BPA monomer entry in GB 4806.6 (plastic resins) was not checked.
9. Health Canada: the pTDI is cited secondhand through Health Canada authors' papers [S25] [S27], not from a Health Canada policy page.
10. Thermal paper analog restrictions (for example a BPS restriction) beyond REACH entry 66 were not researched.

### 6.1 Notes on interpretation

- EU 2024/3190 replaces numeric SMLs for BPA with a prohibition plus an analytical detection limit of 1 microgram/kg [S1]. Any ICS threshold aligned to the EU would therefore be framed as "not detected at LOD 1 microgram/kg (method per [S1])", not as an SML.
- EFSA's 2023 TDI of 0.2 ng/kg bw per day [S6] is a health-based guidance value, not a product limit.
